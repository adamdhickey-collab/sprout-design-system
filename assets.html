11 / Approved assets

Approved assets

The register of what the brand's human-authored assets are, who owns them, where the masters live, and what a generative tool is and isn't allowed to do with each one. This page is deliberately not the library — it holds a few representative examples per category and points at the source of truth. The rule it encodes: Sprout carries the rules and the canonical marks; the asset library carries the inventory; a generative tool composes with both and invents neither.

What belongs where · three layers, one system

Layer responsibilities
Layer Holds Examples
Sprout Rules a machine can follow, plus the canonical marks Tokens, components, spacing, accessibility, UI icons, image ratios, illustration and diagram construction rules, logo and leaf masters, alt-text guidance
Asset library Approved human-created source files Photography, character illustration, custom glyphs, campaign masters, licensed stock, motion, textures
Generative tooling Assembly and exploration — never origination of brand artwork Pages, decks, prototypes, social graphics, alternative compositions built from the two layers above

The failure mode this guards against is a plausible default: a tool asked to fill a gap will fill it, and what comes back is generic — familiar palettes, rounded rectangles, display serifs, compositions that could belong to anyone. Every record below therefore states a prohibition as explicitly as it states a permission, because the permission alone is what leaves the gap open.

Register

Asset records

One record per category. Canonical masters live in this repo and are never regenerated. Approved sets are human-authored and complete enough to use as-is. Placeholder means a stand-in is standing where a licensed library belongs. Not stocked means no approved artwork exists yet — and that a substitute is worse than an omission.

The JSON is the source of truth for this page — the cards below are rendered from it. Copy it into a generative tool's project context to hand over the whole governance record in one paste.

Honest gaps

What this register doesn't know yet

Three fields in every record are governance metadata this repo cannot source on its own. They are filled in with role-level answers where the site already names one, and left explicitly unset where it doesn't — an unset field is a question for Brand Development, not a value to be guessed at.

Source-library location
Where the master files actually live for the categories stored outside this repo. Recorded as unset until a DAM location or share is designated. Anything pointing into this repo is a real path and can be trusted.
Asset owner
Recorded at role level — Cargill Brand Development for brand-owned artwork, Sprout Project Team for system-owned rules and tokens. Named individuals are deliberately not invented here.
Licensing status
Stated where the repo can substantiate it (trademarked marks, licensed typefaces, PDF-extracted artwork). For a photography or stock library that hasn't landed, the licensing terms travel with the library and are recorded as unset.

Selection principles are a genuine gap, not an oversight. The brand book's photography chapter supplies the three categories used throughout this system — Our people, Our systems, Our world — and the leaf treatments that photography has to survive, both recorded below. What it does not supply in a form this repo can cite is the aesthetic doctrine: what makes a frame feel like Cargill rather than like stock. That judgement is exactly the kind this register exists to carry, so it is marked as missing rather than filled with a plausible-sounding paragraph.
